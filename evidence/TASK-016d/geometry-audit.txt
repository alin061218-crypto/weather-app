TASK-016d geometry audit
=========================

Scope: the ten icons in icon.weather. The shared weather SVG uses stroke-width=1.5,
so the review threshold is 1.5 × stroke-width = 2.25 viewBox units. Gaps are
estimated from the closest visible edges in the 24 × 24 coordinate system. A
gap below the threshold is listed even when it is an intentional attachment.

Historical estimates, retained from the 2026-10-06 sun-unification decision.
Superseded on 2026-10-18: clearNight now uses a solid moon and two stars. The remaining
non-crescent estimates below are retained from the previous review; they were
not remeasured for the current night-icon change. The current clearNight contract
is checked by night-icon.cjs and browser-computed SVG bounds in screenshots.cjs.

icon          closest gap   threshold   judgement
------------  ------------  ----------  ----------------------------------------------
clearDay           4.50        2.25      pass; ray-to-sun spacing is deliberate
partlyCloudy       2.50        2.25      pass; cloud and sun are layered, not touching
cloudy             2.50        2.25      pass; cloud/sun overlap is open and readable
overcast           2.00        2.25      intentional; baseline is a separate ground cue
fog                3.50        2.25      pass; horizontal bands remain distinct
rainLight          2.50        2.25      pass; sparse drops remain detached
rainHeavy          1.50        2.25      intentional; dense rain is the weather cue
snow               2.50        2.25      pass; snowflake arms are one connected mark
storm              0.00        2.25      intentional; lightning is attached to the cloud

The only sub-threshold gaps are overcast's cloud-to-baseline separation,
rainHeavy's closest drop-to-cloud start, and storm's attached lightning. They
are intentional construction cues rather than strokes filling an unintended
hole. None is a hidden enclosed gap that collapses at 24px.
