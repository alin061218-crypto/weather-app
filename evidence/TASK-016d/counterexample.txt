TASK-016d geometry guard counterexample
======================================

Command:
  node evidence/TASK-016d/icon-geometry.cjs --inner-radius=5
Exit: 1

Output:
  AssertionError [ERR_ASSERTION]: crescent thickness 9.551 must stay <= 4.200 to avoid a heavy crescent

The --inner-radius=5 switch applies the old inner arc radius to the parsed
clearNight path without changing the checked source file. The same parser and
assertions therefore reject the old, over-thick geometry. The normal command
`node evidence/TASK-016d/icon-geometry.cjs` passes with thickness 3.824 and a
minimum contract of 3.750 (2.5 × stroke-width).
